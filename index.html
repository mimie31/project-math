🌱 Bio-Deriv: Tilapia Growth Rate

Field: SFM (Food Security) - Analyzing Oreochromis niloticus (Tilapia) maturation in Perak aquaculture ponds.

The Function $f(x)$

Predicts biomass (grams) over time (days):

f(x) = L / (1 + e^(-k(x-x₀)))

The Derivative $f'(x)$

Predicts instantaneous growth (g/day):

f'(x) = k · f(x) · (1 - f(x)/L)
Problem: At what point does the Tilapia reach "Peak Growth Velocity"? Identifying this helps farmers optimize feed conversion ratios (FCR) to ensure Malaysian food security.
